# Document 02: Business Hypothesis Register & ICP Framework

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Segment: The Growth-Stage Digital Agency Leader
- **Company Size**: 10–50 full-time employees (Engineering, Design, Project Management).
- **Annual Revenue**: $1,500,000 – $8,000,000 ARR / service billings.
- **Contract Type**: Predominantly fixed-price milestones ($25,000 to $150,000 per engagement) or capped time-and-materials.
- **Key Decision Makers**:
  - Founder / Managing Partner / CEO
  - VP / Director of Delivery & Operations
  - Head of Client Solutions / Bid Manager
- **Psychographic Profile**: Highly sensitive to margin erosion; scarred by past client disputes where the agency worked 200+ unbilled hours to preserve the relationship; eager to standardize sales-to-delivery handoffs.

---

## 2. Core Problem & Job To Be Done (JTBD)

### The Core Problem
Under pressure to close enterprise deals quickly, agency sales and account executives present proposals with ambiguous deliverables, unbounded revision clauses, and subjective acceptance criteria. When clients exploit these loopholes during execution, the agency faces a toxic binary choice: absorb tens of thousands in unbilled rework, or risk contract cancellation and reputation damage.

### Job To Be Done (JTBD)
> *"When we are preparing a client proposal or signing a Statement of Work, we want to instantly audit the contractual terms for margin bleed and scope creep loopholes, so that we can protect our 40%+ gross profit margin, eliminate unbilled revision cycles, and close deals with watertight acceptance rules."*

---

## 3. Solution & Value Proposition

### Current Alternatives
1. **The Gut Check**: Founder skims the proposal 10 minutes before the pitch call.
2. **The 30-Page Word Template**: Outdated boilerplate document with obsolete clauses copied from 2019.
3. **External Lawyer**: $450/hour, 7-business-day turnaround time, overly conservative legal jargon that scares clients away.
4. **Post-Mortem Regret**: Discovering scope loopholes only after the client refuses to sign off on the 3rd revision cycle.

### Proposed Solution: DealForge AI
A browser-based intelligence platform where agency leaders paste or upload their draft Statement of Work (SOW), RFP response, or master agreement. DealForge AI parses the document against 50+ agency-tested risk patterns in <60 seconds, generates a **Margin Risk Score (0–100)**, calculates **Estimated Financial Bleed ($)**, and provides **one-click copyable protective amendment clauses**.

### Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
> **"Audit your agency proposals in 60 seconds. Eliminate scope creep, protect project margins, and never work unbilled hours again."**

---

## 4. Initial Distribution & Pricing Hypotheses

### Initial Distribution Channels
1. **Programmatic Teardowns & LinkedIn Thought Leadership**: Breaking down famous failed public agency contracts (e.g., healthcare portal overruns) highlighting the exact missing clauses.
2. **Direct Cold Outreach & Audits**: Offering agency founders a free "1-minute contract vulnerability check" on an anonymized past SOW.
3. **Agency Communities & Partnerships**: Micro-sponsorships and workshops in Agencynomics, Agency Hackers, and 2B2 Agency Slack communities.

### Pricing Hypotheses
- **Free Tier**: 2 proposal audits / month; basic risk score and top 3 flagged issues.
- **Pro Tier**: $79 / month ($69/mo billed annually); unlimited audits, exportable client reports, custom agency clause library, multi-seat team access.
- **Lifetime Founder Tier**: $499 one-time payment; 3 years guaranteed Pro access, founder onboarding call, priority feature requests (limited to first 50 agency customers).

---

## 5. Three Riskiest Assumptions

1. **Assumption 1 (Workflow / Behavior)**: Agency founders and delivery heads will trust an automated tool to evaluate proposal and contract language without requiring human lawyer certification.
2. **Assumption 2 (Monetization)**: Agencies will pay $79/month recurring for a software utility rather than relying on one-off free templates or chat prompts.
3. **Assumption 3 (Security / Privacy)**: Agency decision-makers will be comfortable pasting proposal drafts into a web app if they are assured zero client PII storage and zero LLM training on their confidential text.

---

## 6. Conditions That Invalidate the Concept

The venture will be declared **INVALIDATED (STOP / PIVOT)** if:
1. Less than 20% of interviewed agency leaders report experiencing >$10k margin loss from scope creep in the trailing 12 months.
2. Customer discovery indicates that contracts are 100% written and mandated by enterprise client legal teams with zero openness to agency amendments.
3. Over 70% of prospective buyers refuse to use the platform unless an in-house attorney signs off on each output.
