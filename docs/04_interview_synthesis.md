## 2. Qualitative Thematic Analysis

### Theme 1: Ambiguous Acceptance Criteria Delaying Cash Flow
- **Observations**: 5 out of 5 participants (100%).
- **Sentiment**: Extremely Negative / High Frustration.
- **Pain Intensity**: **9.6 / 10.0**
- **Supporting Quote (P-02)**: *"We completed 98% of the Shopify build, but because the contract said 'to the client's sole satisfaction', their procurement team froze the final $28,500 invoice for over two months until we did 40 hours of free custom scripts."*
- **Product Implication**: Engine must detect subjective phrases like *"to client satisfaction"*, *"as deemed necessary"*, and *"industry standard performance"* and replace them with objective 5-day deemed acceptance clauses.
- **Confidence Level**: High (Verified by secondary industry benchmarks).

### Theme 2: Founder / Delivery Review Bottlenecks
- **Observations**: 4 out of 5 participants (80%).
- **Sentiment**: Negative / Fatigue.
- **Pain Intensity**: **8.8 / 10.0**
- **Supporting Quote (P-01)**: *"Sales wants to send the proposal before 5 PM to close the quarter. I'm reviewing a 40-page SOW on my phone while boarding a flight. You inevitably miss the uncapped liability clause on page 28."*
- **Product Implication**: Scan speed must be under 60 seconds with an executive summary highlight cards (Risk Score + Top 3 Dangerous Loopholes).
- **Confidence Level**: High.

### Theme 3: Data Privacy & Client Confidentiality Concerns
- **Observations**: 5 out of 5 participants (100%).
- **Sentiment**: Cautious / Non-negotiable requirement.
- **Pain Intensity**: **9.0 / 10.0**
- **Supporting Quote (P-03)**: *"If our enterprise clients found out their proprietary architectural specs were uploaded into an unvetted public AI model, we would be in breach of our NDA."*
- **Product Implication**: Implement local client-side entity redaction (company names, prices, IP) and publish clear zero-data-retention security guarantees.
- **Confidence Level**: Very High.
