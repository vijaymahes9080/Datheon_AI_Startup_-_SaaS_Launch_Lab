# Document 03: Customer Discovery Interview Protocol & Research Methodology

## 1. Research Objectives & Principles
This protocol operationalizes customer discovery under strict **Mom Test** standards:
1. **Never ask if someone likes an idea**: People lie to be polite.
2. **Focus on past concrete behavior, not future promises**: Ask about specific recent contracts, real financial losses, and actual hours spent.
3. **Investigate workarounds and budgets**: If they haven't actively tried to solve the problem with time or money, it is not a top-3 priority.

---

## 2. Participant Screening & Recruitment Plan

### Screening Criteria
- **Role**: Founder, Co-Founder, Managing Partner, VP Delivery, or Head of Client Services.
- **Company**: B2B agency, software consultancy, product studio, or IT service firm.
- **Size**: 8–60 billable employees.
- **Pricing Model**: Actively delivers fixed-fee or hybrid-milestone client projects.
- **Disqualifiers**: Freelancers with zero subcontractors; pure SaaS businesses; staff augmentation brokers with zero deliverable liability.

### Recruitment Channels
1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator Outreach**: 35 personalized messages to agency founders citing shared industry connections.
2. **Agency Communities**: Slack outreach in Agencynomics and Agency Builders.
3. **Warm Network**: Peer founders within startup accelerator networks.

### Informed Consent & Recording Script
> *"Thank you for taking 20 minutes with me today, [Name]. We are conducting research into contract management and project margin predictability for growth-stage agencies. I will not be selling or pitching anything today. Everything you share will be strictly anonymized and aggregated into our industry findings. Do I have your permission to record this session solely for note-taking and qualitative analysis?"*

## 3. Five-Part Behavioral Interview Protocol

### Part 1: Context & Baseline Workflow (3 minutes)
- *"Can you walk me through how your team currently prepares, prices, and reviews a $50k+ client Statement of Work (SOW) before sending it to the client?"*
- *"Who writes the scope? Who reviews the legal and financial terms before signature?"*

### Part 2: Concrete Problem Investigation (6 minutes)
- *"Think back to the last project where scope creep got out of hand or project margins collapsed. What happened?"*
- *"When during the project lifecycle did you first realize the scope had drifted?"*
- *"What was the exact financial or team impact of that dispute (e.g., lost hours, delayed invoice, refunded fees)?"*
- *"How frequently does this kind of misalignment occur across your annual engagements?"*

### Part 3: Workarounds & Current Alternatives (4 minutes)
- *"What have you put in place to prevent that from happening again?"*
- *"Have you ever hired external legal counsel to review your proposal templates or client-provided contracts? What was that experience like in terms of cost and turnaround time?"*
- *"What is your biggest frustration with standard proposal software like PandaDoc or Proposify when it comes to contractual safety?"*

### Part 4: Economic Quantification & Decision Triggers (4 minutes)
- *"Roughly how much money or billable time do you estimate your agency wrote off in the last 12 months due to scope ambiguity?"*
- *"If a tool could scan your draft proposal in 60 seconds and redline dangerous scope loopholes, who on your team would be responsible for running it?"*
- *"What budget would that come out of (software subscriptions, legal, overhead)?"*

### Part 5: Concrete Next Steps & Commitment Test (3 minutes)
- *"We are building a private beta cohort of 10 agency founders who will run anonymized past SOWs through our risk engine to benchmark their margin safety. Would you be open to providing one past redacted contract for our initial audit run?"*
- *(A user who commits time or data demonstrates real demand; a user who merely says 'sounds cool' fails the validation gate).*
