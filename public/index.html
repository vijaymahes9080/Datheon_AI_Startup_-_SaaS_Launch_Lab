Why Fixed-Fee Contracts Bleed Agency Profits

Agencies write off an average of $35,000 per year not because they cannot build, but because sales proposals contain hidden contractual loopholes.

⏳

The 47-Day Milestone Freeze

Vague clauses like "to client satisfaction" allow enterprise procurement to delay your final 20% invoice while demanding free out-of-scope micro-features.

🔄

The Unlimited Revision Trap

Failing to define explicit round limits and client turnaround windows leads to 12 revision rounds on what was priced as a 2-week sprint.

⚖️

Uncapped Indemnity Traps

Client-mandated paper contracts often slip in uncapped consequential damage liability that could wipe out your entire annual company margin.
